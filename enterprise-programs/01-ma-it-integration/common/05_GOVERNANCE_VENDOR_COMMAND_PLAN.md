## Governance model

- **Executive Sponsor / Steering Committee:** owns deal outcomes, funding, target state, priority, risk acceptance and gate decisions.
- **Integration Management Office:** owns integrated roadmap/schedule, dependencies, RAID, financial control, decision log, status, benefits and cross-workstream change.
- **Technical Design Authority / Security:** owns architecture, exceptions, security/privacy/records controls and technical acceptance.
- **Workstream leads:** own deliverables, forecasts, evidence, risks, resources and handoff.
- **Business and service owners:** own business readiness, user acceptance, operating model, benefits and source retirement.
- **Vendors/MSPs:** own contracted outputs and evidence; they do not self-accept deliverables.

Use weekly workstream reviews, an integrated dependency/RAID review, financial/vendor review, change authority, technical/security review and executive steering cadence. During cutover, shift to a command bridge with event log, status intervals, incident roles and explicit go/hold/rollback authority.

## Integrated control pack

Maintain charter/business case, scope and assumptions, roadmap or integrated master schedule, WBS/backlog, dependency map, RAID, decision log, change log, RACI, resource plan, budget/forecast/commitments, quality plan, communications/change plan, architecture decisions, vendor register, SOW/deliverable matrix, SLA/KPI scorecard, cutover plan, acceptance evidence, benefits register, transition plan and closeout/lessons.

PMBOK Guide Eighth Edition alignment is visible across governance, scope, schedule, finance, stakeholders, resources and risk, guided by value, holistic thinking, quality, accountable leadership, sustainability and empowered teams. Delivery method is tailored; controls follow risk and decision need.

## Vendor and MSP lifecycle

1. **Due diligence:** legal entity, ownership, financial viability, references, capability, subcontractors, support locations, insurance and conflicts.
2. **Security/privacy/records:** data flows/classification, access, MFA/PAM, logging, incident notification, vulnerabilities, secure development, retention/deletion, residency, subprocessors, BCP/DR and supply-chain risk.
3. **Contract/SOW:** outcomes, deliverables, assumptions, responsibilities, milestones, acceptance criteria, service levels, reporting, rate card, not-to-exceed, change process, credits, IP, confidentiality, audit, transition assistance and termination.
4. **Onboarding:** named contacts/escalations, RACI, access approvals/expiry, environment orientation, change windows, tools, evidence repository and invoicing rules.
5. **Performance:** delivery acceptance, SLA/KPI/KRI, aging actions, incidents, defects, forecast, resource capacity, dependencies, risk and customer satisfaction.
6. **Cutover/hypercare:** named onsite/remote staffing, shifts, bridge attendance, escalation, spares/capacity, rollback support and response times.
7. **Invoice acceptance:** three-way match of contract/SOW, accepted milestone/time evidence and invoice; disputes logged; retainage/holdbacks applied where contracted.
8. **Exit:** revoke access, return/delete data and assets, transfer knowledge/configuration/licenses, settle obligations, preserve records, update contracts/CMDB and record vendor performance.

## Status and escalation rules

- **Green:** on track and acceptance evidence exists.
- **Amber:** controlled variance, exception or decision needs attention.
- **Red:** blocked, unsafe, materially off plan or no-go.
- **Blue:** planned/in progress/informational.
- **Gray:** not started, unknown or awaiting validation.

Color never replaces the underlying numerator/denominator, owner, due date, decision, risk, evidence and confidence. Escalate any security/privacy breach, critical business interruption, uncontrolled privileged access, legal-hold risk, budget/schedule threshold breach, vendor SLA failure or missing go/no-go evidence according to the approved matrix.

## Method tailoring

**Agile:** outcome roadmap, prioritized backlog, release/sprint goals, Definition of Ready/Done, demos, flow/quality metrics and frequent acceptance; fixed legal/security/Day 1 gates remain.

**Predictive / Waterfall:** baselined WBS/IMS/budget, requirements traceability, stage gates, formal change control, earned value where useful, acceptance and phase closure.
