# Procurement and Vendor Management

> **Evidence-confidence key:** [PB-H] public benchmark or authoritative source/high confidence; [RBE-M] range-based estimate/medium confidence; [SA-L] scenario assumption/low confidence; [DC-L] disclosed derived calculation/confidence inherits the weakest input; [UPV] unverified production value/block commitment until approved.


## Procurement packages

- Robot hardware, end effectors, charging, compute, sensors, and safety equipment.
- Freight, rigging, installation, site construction, network, and electrical work.
- Software licenses, cloud/telemetry, remote support, ITSM, and data retention.
- Field-service labor, training, spares, repair, calibration, and RMA logistics.

## SOW minimums

Scope and exclusions; deliverables; milestone dates; roles; site assumptions; acceptance criteria; evidence required; pricing and payment triggers; travel; change control; security/privacy; warranty; service levels; IP/data rights; subcontractors; termination and transition assistance.

## Supplier selection criteria

**Table 1. Supplier selection criteria — Evidence: disclosed row/source notes; Confidence: see evidence key and row/source notes**

| Dimension | Example weight |
|---|---:|
| Technical/use-case fit | 25% |
| Safety, quality, and evidence maturity | 20% |
| Delivery capacity and lead time | 15% |
| Support, field coverage, spares, and warranty | 15% |
| Integration/data/security fit | 10% |
| Total cost and commercial terms | 10% |
| Financial/operational risk | 5% |

## Weekly vendor scorecard

On-time deliverables; open critical defects; first-pass yield; lead-time variance; support SLA; corrective-action aging; forecast accuracy; change-request cycle time; invoice accuracy; customer-impact events.

## Long-lead control

The program manager maintains required-by date, current promise date, float, approved substitute, expediting action, receiving/inspection owner, and impact if missed. Safety-related and single-source components receive explicit contingency plans.

## Acceptance and payment

Payment milestones are tied to accepted deliverables rather than shipment alone. Exceptions identify retained amount, correction date, operational containment, and warranty impact.
