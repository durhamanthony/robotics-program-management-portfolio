# Benefits Realization and Project Closeout

> **Evidence-confidence key:** [PB-H] public benchmark or authoritative source/high confidence; [RBE-M] range-based estimate/medium confidence; [SA-L] scenario assumption/low confidence; [DC-L] disclosed derived calculation/confidence inherits the weakest input; [UPV] unverified production value/block commitment until approved.


## Benefits plan

**Table 1. Benefits plan — Evidence: disclosed row/source notes; Confidence: see evidence key and row/source notes**

| Benefit type | Baseline | Target | Data owner | Review cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Throughput/productivity | Pre-deployment units/hour and labor | Contracted improvement | Customer operations | Weekly then monthly |
| Safety/ergonomics | Recordable events, high-risk exposures, interventions | Approved reduction | Customer EHS | Monthly |
| Availability/reliability | Current downtime and mission completion | Contract/SLA threshold | Service owner | Daily/weekly |
| Quality | Damage, false alert, rework, escape rate | Approved limit | Quality/process owner | Weekly |
| Financial | Current labor/service/TCO | Approved business case | Finance/sponsor | Monthly/quarterly |
| Experience/adoption | Operator/support feedback and workarounds | Adoption and satisfaction target | Change lead | 7/30/60 days |

Benefits are not claimed from simulation results. In a real program, the customer and finance owner approve the baseline, normalization rules, exclusions, and attribution method.

## Handoff checklist

- Accepted configuration and installed-base record.
- Signed acceptance and open-exception register.
- Safety/security approvals and residual-risk ownership.
- Operations, support, field, escalation, remote-access, and RMA procedures.
- Trained staff and authorization records.
- Monitoring, dashboards, SLA/KPI, known errors, spares, warranty, and vendor contacts.
- Financial close, asset capitalization/transfer, contract obligations, and renewal dates.

## Closeout report

Summarize delivered scope, acceptance, schedule/cost outcome, unresolved items, realized/forecast benefits, incidents, quality, customer feedback, lessons, and recommended expansion or corrective roadmap.

## Closure criteria

The project closes only when deliverables are accepted or formally deferred, operational ownership is signed, records are archived, financial/procurement actions are complete, lessons are assigned into standards/backlogs, and a named owner accepts future benefits measurement.
