# Communications and Meeting Plan

## Stakeholder communications

| Audience | Information | Cadence | Owner | Channel/output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive sponsor/steering committee | Outcome, forecast, top risks, decisions, benefits | Monthly and gates | Program manager | One-page status and decision pack |
| Customer/site leadership | Readiness, disruption, safety, training, acceptance | Weekly; daily at launch | Deployment PM | Status report and action log |
| Engineering/product | Requirements, defects, releases, dependencies | Twice weekly | Technical PM | Jira board and technical review |
| Safety/security/privacy | Hazards, controls, evidence, exceptions | Weekly and before gates | Control owner | Review record and approval log |
| Operations/support/field | Runbooks, support model, readiness, incidents | Weekly; daily in hypercare | Service readiness lead | Readiness tracker and handoff pack |
| Operators/frontline staff | What changes, when, training, help path | At 30/14/7/1 days | Change lead | Briefing, FAQ, training, shift huddle |
| Vendors/suppliers | Deliverables, quality, dates, risks, corrective actions | Weekly | Vendor manager | Scorecard and action register |

## Standard meeting architecture

### Weekly program review — 45 minutes

1. Safety and customer-impact check.
2. Milestones versus baseline.
3. Workstream exceptions only.
4. Top risks, issues, dependencies, and changes.
5. Decisions required and action confirmation.
6. Two-week look-ahead.

### Steering committee — 30 minutes

1. Outcome/benefit trend.
2. Overall RAG and forecast.
3. Material changes since last review.
4. Decisions with options, recommendation, impact, and decision-by date.
5. Gate recommendation.

### Launch/hypercare stand-up — 15 minutes

1. Safety incidents and protective stops.
2. Fleet/site status and last-shift performance.
3. Active Sev-1/Sev-2 incidents and customer communications.
4. Blockers, named owners, and today’s go/no-go conditions.

## Communication standard

Every escalation states the observed fact, customer/operational impact, immediate containment, accountable owner, next update time, and decision needed. Avoid unqualified assurances and avoid exposing restricted technical or customer data.
