## Financial reconciliation

Installed pilot spending was $640,000 in the fictional budget. Annual recurring planning cost is $218,000. Annualized value is $96,058 from 4,965 returned hours at the Glassdoor June 2026 U.S. Retail Sales Associate average salary of $40,242/year ($19.35/hour planning equivalent) plus $52,200 fictional measured margin recovery. Gross value is $69,742 below recurring cost and $197,742 below the five-year annual threshold. Five-year shortfall is $988,708. The Glassdoor value is a crowd-sourced research benchmark [RBE-M], not Meridian payroll or total compensation.

Finance therefore recorded **no chain rollout**. No savings were booked from returned time because actual payroll, overtime, vacancies, schedules, and customer transaction effects were not supplied.

## Lessons

1. A humanoid justification must identify the human infrastructure actually used: receiving zones, doors, stairs, aisles, shelves, garment rails, and courtesy drop-off points.
2. Forklift unloading and humanoid stocking require a positive, auditable zone-clear handoff rather than simultaneous mixed traffic.
3. Tangled hangers and damaged packaging belong in a defined exception workflow until tested evidence says otherwise.
4. Operational success is not a return-on-investment result.
5. Planning allowances must not be displayed as market prices.
6. A program manager adds credibility by stopping rollout when the evidence fails the decision threshold.
