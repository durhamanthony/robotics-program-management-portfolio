# Seller / Integrator Project Manager Plan

> **Evidence-confidence key:** [PB-H] public benchmark or authoritative source/high confidence; [RBE-M] range-based estimate/medium confidence; [SA-L] scenario assumption/low confidence; [DC-L] disclosed derived calculation/confidence inherits the weakest input; [UPV] unverified production value/block commitment until approved.


## Accountable outcome

Deliver the contracted two-robot pilot, integrate manufacturer and airport work, produce acceptance evidence, and hand off a supportable four-room service without expanding the approved operating envelope.

## Work packages

1. Contract baseline: deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, milestones, acceptance, warranty, and change pricing.
2. Site discovery: room dimensions, fixture interfaces, traffic windows, network, entry control, dock, supply, and recovery path.
3. Integrated design: identity, task configuration, telemetry, chemical/tool interface, privacy, support, and evidence correlation.
4. Build and Factory Acceptance Test: configured assets, fault injection, evidence pack, shipment release.
5. Installation and Site Acceptance Test: serialized install, as-built record, room-by-room test, punch list.
6. Training and shadow mode: operator, custodian, support, facilities, and safety assessments.
7. Pilot and hypercare: daily control meeting, defect ownership, spare readiness, customer communication.
8. Handoff and closeout: accepted deliverables, cost reconciliation, warranty start, residual-risk owners.

## Seller controls

- One integrated schedule and interface register; no separate unlinked supplier dates.
- Daily defect triage during commissioning; weekly steering report during pilot.
- Configuration identifier attached to every mission and incident.
- Contract change required for every room, task, chemical, or operating-hour expansion.
- Payment only after named acceptance evidence, not percent-complete opinion.

## Completed result

All eight deliverables were accepted. Actual seller/integrator spend was $304,200 against the $310,000 planning work package. Two noncritical punch-list items—cabinet label durability and one charger cable guard—closed before handoff. Warranty and Severity 1/Severity 2 support began at pilot acceptance.
