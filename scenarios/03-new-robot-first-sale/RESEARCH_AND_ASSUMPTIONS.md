## Scenario inputs

- Customer: two handlers per shift, two shifts, 7.5 productive hours, 250 days, with 0.5 handler-equivalent retained per shift.
- Product: one tote family, 2–15 kilograms, 18-meter indoor route, two stations, level floor, 16 scheduled hours per day.
- Offer: $285,000 product, $45,000 install/training, $36,000 annual service.
- Unit economics: $150,000 Production Validation Test Cost of Goods Sold target and $135,000 contribution before sales, support, warranty, corporate overhead, working capital, tax, and financing.
- Development: 20 average Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) team members for 18 months at $162,500 average annual loaded cost.

## Replacement evidence

Observed customer time study; actual payroll and contractor rates; twelve customer interviews and four design-partner commitments; product architecture; hazard analysis; vendor quotations; Bill of Materials; prototype plan; certification/legal/insurance review; reliability plan; production labor study; cloud/storage forecast; deployment site survey; signed commercial terms; and demand forecast.

No value is assigned to injury avoidance, overtime reduction, downstream throughput, or customer revenue. Those benefits require customer evidence and finance approval.
