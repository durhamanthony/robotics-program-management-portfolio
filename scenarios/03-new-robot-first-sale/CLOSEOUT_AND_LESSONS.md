# Closeout and Lessons — AD-01 First Sale

**Manufacturer:** Axiom Dynamics  
**First customer:** HarborPoint Fulfillment  
**Program duration:** 18 months  
**Final decision:** Limited availability approved for the released two-station workflow  
**Development authorization / final spend:** $10,975,000 / $10,975,000

## Product and first-customer results

| Release measure | Gate | Final result | Disposition |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Design Verification Test mission success | At least 98% over 2,000 cycles | 98.4% after thermal correction | Pass |
| Production Validation Test first-pass yield | At least 90% | 9 of 10 units (90%) | Pass |
| Released Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) | No more than $150,000 | $149,200 | Pass |

| First-customer interventions | No more than 3 per 100 missions | 2.1 per 100 | Pass |

## First-sale completion

- HarborPoint signed the 30-day acceptance report and acknowledged the released operating envelope: one approved tote type, up to 15 kilograms, an 18-meter indoor flat-floor route, two mapped stations, and supervised exception recovery.
- Installed hardware, software, calibration, configuration, operating limits, training records, warranty, spares, and support ownership match the released baseline.
- Production Validation Test records, supplier deviations, Cost of Goods Sold, inventory, and development invoices were reconciled.
- One residual scale risk remains: the actuator second source must complete qualification before General Availability.

## Release decision

Leadership approved **limited availability**, not General Availability. Sales may offer the common two-station configuration without customer-specific engineering. General Availability requires actuator second-source qualification and a qualified commercial pipeline capable of supporting the 82-unit development break-even screen.

## Lessons carried forward

1. A narrow product boundary made safety, cost, and customer value testable.
2. Serviceability, fault codes, and telemetry must be product requirements.
3. A supplier response needs a dated qualification gate, owner, and buffer—not a generic mitigation.
4. Contract language must match the demonstrated operating envelope.
5. One accepted first customer does not establish broad product-market fit.
