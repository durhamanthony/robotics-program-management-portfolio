# Commercial Launch Plan — AD-01

## Offer definition

Publish the supported workflow, payload/layout/environment envelope, exclusions, customer dependencies, site/readiness lead time, deployment package, training, warranty, Service-Level Agreement (SLA), spares, data/security terms, pricing, acceptance method, and change mechanism.

## Launch readiness

| Function | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Product/engineering | Released baseline, limitations, roadmap, defect disposition |
| Manufacturing/supply | Costed Bill of Materials (BOM), yield/capacity, traceability, shipment readiness |
| Quality/safety/security | Mandatory approvals, evidence file, incident/recall paths |
| Sales/solutions | Qualification guide, approved claims, demo, pricing/configurator |
| Legal/finance | Contract/Statement of Work (SOW), acceptance, warranty, data, insurance, revenue policy |
| Deployment/service | Site survey, install/runbooks, training, cases, on-call, spares/Return Material Authorization (RMA) |
| Customer success | Adoption plan, Key Performance Indicator (KPI) baseline, Quarterly Business Review (QBR), renewal/expansion criteria |

## First-customer rule

Do not disguise product gaps as customer-specific implementation. Any exception is visible in contract, configuration, risk, schedule, test, support, and economics. General availability requires repeatability evidence, not only a signed first sale.
