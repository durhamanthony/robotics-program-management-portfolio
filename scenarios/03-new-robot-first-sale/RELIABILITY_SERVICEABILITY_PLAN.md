# Reliability and Serviceability Plan — AD-01

Development gates are Engineering Verification Test (EVT), Design Verification Test (DVT), and Production Validation Test (PVT).

## Objectives

Design service evidence and repair paths before shipment; quantify failures through representative duty cycles; and turn field experience into controlled product improvement.

## Program

| Phase | Reliability/serviceability work | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Concept/EVT | Failure-mode analysis, mission profile, critical assumptions, instrumentation | Risk-prioritized test plan |
| DVT | Environmental/load/life testing, fault injection, maintainability demonstrations | Reliability growth and defect disposition |
| PVT | Process variation, end-of-line coverage, golden unit, service tooling/FRUs | Release/service readiness |
| First customer | Telemetry review, incident/problem loop, spare use, repair time | 30-day performance report |

## Required service design

Unique serial and configuration; human-readable faults; synchronized MCAP robotics-log evidence; secure diagnostic access; Field-Replaceable Units (FRUs); service manuals; safe isolation; calibration and return-to-service test; spare recommendation; repair/Return Material Authorization (RMA) flow; escalation contract; rollback; known-error database.

## Measures

Availability, mission success, interventions/100 missions, failure rate by subsystem, mean time to diagnose/repair, first-time fix, repeat repair, no-fault-found returns, spare fill, warranty cost/robot/month, and high-severity incident recurrence.
