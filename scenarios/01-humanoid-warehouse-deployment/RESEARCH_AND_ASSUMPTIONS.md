# Research and Assumptions — Warehouse Truck Loading

## Source labels used in the financial model

- **Public benchmark:** a dated figure or capability from a government or manufacturer source. It is an anchor, not customer truth.
- **Scenario contract input:** a fictitious value representing information that would come from an executed order or customer baseline.
- **Quote-required allowance:** a planning value that cannot become an approval baseline until a named supplier or customer provides a quote.
- **Calculated:** arithmetic from labeled inputs; formulas remain visible in the artifact.

## Public evidence used

| Topic | Source | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material-moving wages | [BLS May 2025 wage overview](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2025/may/largest2.htm) | National wage reasonableness | Customer wage or staffing level |
| Loaded compensation | [BLS March 2026 occupational compensation](https://www.bls.gov/charts/employer-costs-for-employee-compensation/costs-by-occupational-group.htm) | $36.27/hour public labor-cost anchor | Customer overtime, agency markup, or severance |
| Warehouse ergonomics | [OSHA warehousing hazards](https://www.osha.gov/warehousing/hazards-solutions) | Repetitive material-handling risk factors | Monetized injury savings |
| Lifting-risk inputs | [NIOSH Revised Lifting Equation](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/ergonomics/about/rnle.html) | Required ergonomic study inputs | A pass/fail result without a site study |
| Robotics safety | [OSHA robotics standards](https://www.osha.gov/robotics/standards) | Risk assessment and system-integration references | Certification of this scenario |
| Human-space deployment concept | [Agility logistics product description](https://www.agilityrobotics.com/content/agility-robotics-launches-next-generation-of-digit-worlds-first-human-centric-multi-purpose-robot-made-for-logistics-work) | Humanoid form factor is intended for existing logistics spaces | Price or performance of the scenario robots |

## Values that must be replaced before a real investment decision

Executed robot order; end-effector and charging quote; supported carton and pallet specification; measured manual process hours; actual wages/benefits and temporary-labor invoices; facility and network quote; insurance/tax/financing; software/support price; spare consumption; reliability; support labor; production volume; accepted throughput; damage and intervention baseline; and Finance's discount rate and benefit-recognition rules.

No claimed savings are attributed to safety or injury reduction. That would require a qualified ergonomic assessment, incident baseline, corrective-action plan, and Finance-approved valuation.
