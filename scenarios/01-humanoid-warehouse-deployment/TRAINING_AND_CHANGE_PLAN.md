# Training and Change Plan — Warehouse

## Stakeholders and change impacts

Operators gain mission/start-stop and exception duties; supervisors gain production and escalation decisions; maintenance gains approved inspections and recovery; IT/security gains identity/network/remote-access ownership; support gains evidence and triage duties; EHS owns the operating-boundary and incident process.

## Curriculum

| Audience | Learning objective | Method | Pass evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operators | Preflight, mission flow, approved intervention, stop/escalate | Demonstration + hands-on | Observation checklist and quiz |
| Supervisors | Go/no-go, KPI, staffing, exception and incident command | Scenario workshop | Tabletop assessment |
| Maintenance/field | Isolation boundaries, inspection, FRU workflow, return-to-service | Lab + supervised practical | Skills signoff |
| L1/L2 support | Intake, safe response, logs, case linking, escalation | Tool lab + drill | End-to-end ticket drill |
| IT/security | Network, identity, access, certificate, audit, retention | Technical handoff | Control checklist |

## Adoption plan

Brief affected workers before installation; publish role-based job aids; use champions on each shift; conduct shadow operation before production; hold daily floor feedback during pilot; track training completion, interventions, confidence, and recurring confusion. Refresh training after material configuration or procedure changes.
