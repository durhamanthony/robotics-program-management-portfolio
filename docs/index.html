CASE 01Amber

Two Humanoids — Retail Backroom Fulfillment

Deploy two humanoid robots in an unchanged, employee-only department-store backroom. A sales associate submits a stock-keeping unit, size, and color request from a tablet. A robot walks through existing human aisles and doors, uses the short stair route where required, retrieves a shoe box or already-hung garment, and delivers it to an employee handoff point. The same fleet performs put-away and cycle-count work between requests. Tangled hangers, ladders, electrical work, and customer-floor operation remain human tasks.

20 weeks including 30-day pilot$640K pilot installedRetrieval mission success: 98.1%

CASE 02Green

Three Quadrupeds — Night Security

Deploy three quadruped robots for human-supervised night patrol across stairs, curbs, gravel, and narrow walkways. Two robots run scheduled perimeter routes while the third rotates through charging and reserve coverage. Robots observe and report; authorized human security personnel retain all response decisions.

CASE 03Amber

AD-01 Autonomous Tote Transfer Robot — First Sale

Develop a compact mobile manipulator that moves one approved tote type weighing up to 15 kilograms along an 18-meter indoor route between two fixed stations. The program runs from observed customer workflows through Engineering Verification Test, Design Verification Test, Production Validation Test, first-customer deployment, 30-day acceptance, and a commercial release decision.

CASE 05Amber

Two Humanoids — Airport Restroom Cleaning Pilot

Deploy two humanoid robots into four unchanged Terminal 4 public restrooms for controlled routine cleaning and replenishment. The rooms remain closed and verified empty during robot work. Airport custodians retain inspection, prohibited-contamination response, corrective cleaning, supply custody, and reopening authority. The program is managed through three coordinated viewpoints: seller/integrator, humanoid manufacturer, and airport buyer/operator.
