CASE 01Amber

Two Humanoids — Retail Backroom Fulfillment

Deploy two humanoid robots across the employee-only retail backroom flow. During inbound receiving, a robot-operated forklift visibly carries a loaded pallet from the delivery truck, places it in the center receiving zone, and returns into the truck; humanoids then carry cartons to lower and raised storage racks, using the visible stair route where required. During fulfillment, a sales associate submits a stock-keeping unit, size, and color request from a tablet; robots pull the requested merchandise, place it on the courtesy drop-off table, and turn toward the shelves while a human associate bends connected arms to collect both packages through the staffed service window, turns around, and walks away. Tangled hangers, damaged packaging, ladders, electrical work, and customer-floor operation remain human tasks.
