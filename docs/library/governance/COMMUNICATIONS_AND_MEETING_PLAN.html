Standard meeting architecture

Weekly program review — 45 minutes

  1. Safety and customer-impact check.
  2. Milestones versus baseline.
  3. Workstream exceptions only.
  4. Top risks, issues, dependencies, and changes.
  5. Decisions required and action confirmation.
  6. Two-week look-ahead.

Steering committee — 30 minutes

  1. Outcome/benefit trend.
  2. Overall RAG and forecast.
  3. Material changes since last review.
  4. Decisions with options, recommendation, impact, and decision-by date.
  5. Gate recommendation.

Launch/hypercare stand-up — 15 minutes

  1. Safety incidents and protective stops.
  2. Fleet/site status and last-shift performance.
  3. Active Sev-1/Sev-2 incidents and customer communications.
  4. Blockers, named owners, and today’s go/no-go conditions.

Communication standard

Every escalation states the observed fact, customer/operational impact, immediate containment, accountable owner, next update time, and decision needed. Avoid unqualified assurances and avoid exposing restricted technical or customer data.
