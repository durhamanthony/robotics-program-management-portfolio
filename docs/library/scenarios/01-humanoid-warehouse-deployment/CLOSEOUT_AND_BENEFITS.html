Closeout and Benefits — Warehouse Truck-Loading Deployment

Customer: Northstar Distribution Center
Program duration: 24 weeks
Closeout decision: Accepted for controlled two-shift operation
Financial authorization / final spend: $1,346,000 / $1,346,000

Final acceptance record

Measure Contract threshold Final result Disposition
Mandatory safety tests 100%; no open critical finding 100%; no open critical finding Pass
Sustained throughput At least 225 cartons/hour for two hours 231 cartons/hour Pass
Pick-carry-place mission success At least 98% 98.6% Pass
Human interventions No more than 2 per 100 cartons 1.4 per 100 cartons Pass
Damage or wrong placement No more than 0.5% 0.3% Pass
Fleet availability during 14-day hypercare At least 95% 96.2% Pass
End-to-end material-flow cycles 30 witnessed cycles 30 completed Pass

The witnessed cycles included conveyor stop position, carton receipt, lane-separated travel, seven-carton pallet completion, robot pause, forklift truck entry, empty-forklift return, replacement-pallet placement, and authorized restart.

Handoff and administrative closure

  • Northstar Operations accepted the two-shift operating procedure, supported-carton envelope, reject workflow, pallet-exchange control, and staffing plan.
  • Robot serial numbers, released software/configuration, warranties, onsite spares, access roles, and training records were transferred to the service owner.
  • The telemetry-to-case, escalation, field-action, and authorized return-to-service drill passed.
  • Purchase orders, invoices, accruals, and approved change records were reconciled. No unapproved scope remained.
  • Residual carton-mix and spare-lead-time risks were transferred to named operations and service owners.

Benefits decision

The technical deployment is accepted. A second cell is not automatically authorized. Finance must validate at least $445,200 in annual gross capacity value using Northstar's actual wage, overtime, temporary-labor, volume, and redeployment records. Below that value, the five-year simple-payback gate fails.

Lessons carried forward

  1. Freeze a representative carton and exception set before the Factory Acceptance Test (FAT).
  2. Make trailer and dock wireless coverage a dated site-readiness gate.
  3. Put support evidence requirements into the released product configuration.
  4. Train abnormal recovery and pallet exchange, not only normal operation.
  5. Base expansion on measured operations and Finance-approved capacity value, not animation speed.
