Daily operating review

Availability, missions/cartons, mission success, interventions, exceptions, damage, stops, battery/charging, alerts/incidents, open defects, parts, staffing/training, and safety observations.

Handoff exit criteria

  • Accepted asset/configuration and document set.
  • Customer operators and vendor support personnel trained and assessed.
  • Support portal, contacts, severity, on-call, remote access, work orders, and Return Material Authorization (RMA) tested.
  • Minimum onsite spares/tools verified.
  • Dashboards and data ownership validated.
  • Maintenance and controlled recovery procedures approved.
  • Known errors, workarounds, open risks, and time-bounded waivers accepted.
  • Benefits owner and 30/60/90-day review scheduled.
