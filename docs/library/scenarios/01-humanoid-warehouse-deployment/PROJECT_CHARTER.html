Project Charter — Humanoid Pallet-Building and Truck-Loading Deployment

  • Sponsor: VP Customer Operations / customer distribution executive
  • Program manager: Robotics Deployment Program Manager
  • Fleet/site: Five humanoids, five conveyor-fed work lanes, one warehouse, phased shift coverage
  • Planned duration: 24 weeks from kickoff through hypercare exit

In scope

Use-case and site validation; five robot configurations; charging; marked robot lanes; conveyor/WMS (Warehouse Management System) interface; pallet exchange; robot-operated forklift and truck interface; network and identity; telemetry; operator/support training; FAT (Factory Acceptance Test); SAT (Site Acceptance Test); UAT (User Acceptance Testing); safety-validation coordination; spares; support; pilot; go-live; hypercare; and handoff.

Out of scope

Warehouse building modification beyond approved readiness work; cartons outside the validated operating envelope; autonomous trailer movement; handling hazardous/leaking goods; unsupervised expansion to other lanes/sites; claims of safety certification by the program manager.

Milestones

Milestone Target week Exit evidence
Scope/acceptance baseline 2 Signed crosswalk and assumptions log
Use-case/site feasibility 4 Survey, process data, risk pre-assessment
Design/configuration baseline 8 Approved interfaces, safety concept, support design
FAT (Factory Acceptance Test) complete 13 48-test report passed; defects dispositioned
Site ready / ship authorization 15 Readiness certificate and go/no-go
SAT (Site Acceptance Test), safety validation, and UAT (User Acceptance Testing) complete 19 Evidence package and conditional acceptance
Production go-live 21 Go-live approval
Hypercare exit/handoff 24 KPIs met and operations signoff

Governance

Daily workstream stand-up during build/onsite; weekly program review; biweekly steering committee; immediate escalation for safety/security events, acceptance-blocking defects, critical-path variance over five business days, or forecast budget variance over 10%.
