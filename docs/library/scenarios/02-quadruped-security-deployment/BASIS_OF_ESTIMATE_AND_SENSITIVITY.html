Basis of Estimate and Sensitivity — Quadruped Security

Evidence classes

Input Base value Evidence class What replaces it in a real project
Three quadruped base platforms $300,000 Public list-price anchor Binding configuration quote, freight, tax, customs, warranty, and support terms
Payloads, docks, networking, integration, training, spares, and reserve $430,250 Fictional planning allowances Approved Bill of Materials, site design, integrator proposal, and risk-priced estimate
Annual fleet software, support, maintenance, spares, and evidence storage $117,000 Fictional planning allowance Vendor subscription, service-level, storage, egress, maintenance, and spares quotes
Walking-rover contract rate $45 per hour Fictional customer input Executed guard-services invoice and contract-change terms
Patrol coverage Two posts × 10 hours × 365 nights Fictional customer input Approved staffing roster, post orders, and observed coverage study
Avoided patrol vehicle $15,000 per year Fictional customer input Fleet ledger and finance approval

The $100,000-per-platform anchor comes from a public Unitree B2 listing. The listing directs buyers to contact sales and does not establish the configured security-system price or suitability. The portfolio does not treat the remaining installed cost as a vendor fact.

Reproducible calculations

  • Installed planning cost = $300,000 platforms + $430,250 other launch allowances = $730,250.
  • Annual recurring planning cost = $117,000.
  • Base gross annual value = 2 posts × 10 hours × 365 nights × $45 + $15,000 vehicle = $343,500.
  • Base annual net value = $343,500 − $117,000 = $226,500.
  • Base simple payback = $730,250 ÷ $226,500 = 3.22 years.
  • Five-year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) = $730,250 + 5 × $117,000 = $1,315,250.
  • Five-year net benefit = 5 × $343,500 − $1,315,250 = $402,250.

Decision sensitivity

Case Posts displaced Gross annual value Net annual value Simple payback Decision
Base 2 $343,500 $226,500 3.22 years Passes five-year screen
One-post 1 $179,250 $62,250 11.73 years Fails
No labor displacement 0 $15,000 ($102,000) None Fails

The five-year payback threshold is $263,050 in gross annual value: $117,000 recurring cost plus $730,250 ÷ 5. The project must not proceed on avoided-loss, deterrence, insurance, or safety value unless the customer documents and Finance approves those benefits.

Estimate confidence

This is a decision model, not a price claim. The arithmetic is checked; the scenario inputs are not procurement evidence. Estimate confidence remains low until competitive quotes, a route study, a contract baseline, privacy and legal approval, and a controlled patrol pilot are complete.
