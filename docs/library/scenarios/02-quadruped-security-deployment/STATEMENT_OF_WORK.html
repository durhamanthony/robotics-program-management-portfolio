Statement of Work — Three-Quadruped Security Deployment

Purpose

Deploy three quadruped robots for scheduled observation patrols on approved nighttime routes at one fenced industrial campus. Robots observe and report; licensed or authorized human security personnel verify alerts and decide any response.

Deliverables

Deliverable Acceptance evidence
Concept of Operations (CONOPS), authority, privacy, and use restrictions Security/legal/privacy approval
Day/night site survey, routes, geofences, docks, and retrieval plan Signed survey and route hazard map
Configured fleet, network, identity, telemetry, alert and evidence interfaces Design and Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) results
Commissioning, shadow patrol, fault and safe-return tests Site Acceptance Test (SAT) and patrol evidence
Operator, supervisor, responder, support, and field training Assessments and drills
Controlled go-live, hypercare, and operations handoff User Acceptance Testing (UAT), Key Performance Indicator (KPI) results, and signoff

Exclusions

Physical confrontation, weapons, pursuit, detention, unsupervised law-enforcement decisions, facial recognition, public-space deployment, or surveillance outside the approved purpose. Any requested capability expansion requires legal, privacy, safety, security, and commercial review.

Customer dependencies

Customer supplies site access, security policy, response staff, network/power, privacy notices, emergency/retrieval support, evidence-system integration, and timely acceptance witnesses. Weather and route operating limits must be explicitly agreed.

Completion

Complete when the 30-night pilot meets the thresholds in the requirements matrix: at least 98% route completion, all 50 witnessed critical alerts delivered within 15 seconds, no more than six operator-reviewed false alerts per 10-hour night after tuning, zero geofence violations, and all mandatory fault, privacy, training, and support tests passed. Open exceptions must be formally dispositioned and operations must accept ownership.
