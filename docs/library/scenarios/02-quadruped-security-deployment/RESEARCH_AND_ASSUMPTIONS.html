Research and Assumptions — Quadruped Night Security

Public capability and price anchors

Topic Source Portfolio use Limitation
Industrial quadruped price Unitree B2 $100,000 public base-platform reference The listing also says to contact sales; no security payload, dock, support, tax, customs, integration, or suitability is assumed
Industrial quadruped terrain Unitree B2 Category feasibility screening for stairs and uneven terrain Manufacturer claim; customer route testing remains mandatory
Quadruped operating envelope Boston Dynamics Spot Independent category reference for slope, step, ingress protection, runtime, payload, and sensing Not a quote and not the selected product
Existing human infrastructure Agility logistics product description Supports the limited claim that legged/human-form robots may reduce retrofit needs Does not mean zero site modification

Privacy risk NIST Privacy Framework Risk-based privacy structure Does not determine local surveillance law
Cybersecurity risk NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 Governance, identification, protection, detection, response, and recovery outcomes Does not prescribe the technical design

Scenario/customer inputs

  • One fenced industrial campus; 10-hour night patrol window; two walking rover posts plus one retained fixed-post human responder.
  • Customer contract invoice of $45 per rover hour; 365-night schedule; one $15,000 annual patrol-vehicle cost that can be avoided.
  • Eight scheduled route circuits per night; two primary patrol robots and one reserve/charging robot.
  • No credit for prevented theft, liability, insurance reduction, or emergency-response value.

Replacement evidence required

Competitive robot and payload quotes; safety and operating limits; battery/runtime test; dock/network/site design; actual guard invoices and contract change rights; labor/legal review; route and radio survey; false-alert baseline; evidence volume and retention; cybersecurity architecture; privacy impact assessment; insurance; maintenance history; spares model; and local legal approval.

The business case intentionally fails when only one rover post can be removed. That sensitivity is a decision control, not a defect in the model.
