Project and Deployment Plan

Evidence-confidence key: [PB-H] public benchmark or authoritative source/high confidence; [RBE-M] range-based estimate/medium confidence; [SA-L] scenario assumption/low confidence; [DC-L] disclosed derived calculation/confidence inherits the weakest input; [UPV] unverified production value/block commitment until approved.

Scope

Three quadrupeds; routes/geofences; docks; network; operator console; approved video/thermal data; alert integration; identity/remote access; telemetry; training; commissioning; patrol acceptance; support/spares/Return Material Authorization (RMA); hypercare.

Explicit exclusions

Facial recognition unless separately approved and lawful; weapons; physical intervention; autonomous law-enforcement decisions; public-space deployment; route changes outside formal review; surveillance beyond the documented purpose and retention policy.

Night-site survey checklist

Route width/slope/surface; drains/holes/curbs/stairs/drop-offs; gates/doors; lighting and glare; rain/fog/dust/temperature; animals; vehicles and pedestrians; restricted/private areas; radio/cellular/Wi-Fi coverage; dock access and power; emergency retrieval; safe manual transport; camera fields; signage and workforce notification.

Cutover progression

  1. Daytime controlled route with escort.
  2. Nighttime controlled route with escort.
  3. One robot/one short segment under operator supervision.
  4. One full route; validate alert and safe-return behavior.
  5. Two robots concurrent; validate coverage, comms, and docking conflicts.
  6. Three-robot operating plan including reserve/charging rotation.
  7. Thirty-night controlled pilot after go/no-go approval; expand only after the thresholds in REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.csv pass.
