RMA, Parts, and Field Service Process

Workflow

  1. Case and remote diagnosis establish the affected asset and suspected FRU.
  2. Entitlement/warranty and safety/shipping constraints are verified.
  3. Field work order reserves technician, tool, part, site window, and return-to-service test.
  4. Failed serialized part is replaced; robot/part configuration records update.
  5. Returned part receives authorization, label, packaging, chain of custody, and depot intake.
  6. Depot records as-found condition, diagnostic result, repair/no-fault-found/scrap/vendor return, and causal code.
  7. Inventory, finance/warranty, customer case, problem record, and engineering defect are linked.
  8. Recurring failures trigger supplier corrective action, design review, or service bulletin.

Inventory controls

Installed base and failure rate; lead time; service level; criticality; repair yield; no-fault-found; obsolescence; battery/hazardous handling; serialized lot/firmware/calibration; min/max by depot/site/vehicle; cycle count; quarantine; recall traceability.

Field performance metrics

Dispatch response, travel, onsite time, repair time, first-time fix, repeat visit, part fill rate, van/depot stockout, RMA cycle time, no-fault-found, repair yield, return-to-service pass rate, safety observations, and cost per visit/robot.
