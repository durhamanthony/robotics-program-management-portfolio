Severity and Service Targets

Targets are planning values and must align with executed Service-Level Agreements (SLAs), product risk, staffing, geography, and safety authority. Severity 1 (S1) is critical, Severity 2 (S2) is high, Severity 3 (S3) is medium, and Severity 4 (S4) is low.

Severity Definition Acknowledge Customer update Restore target Leadership/escalation
S1 Critical Safety event/credible hazard; site/fleet stopped; security/privacy incident; no safe workaround 15 min 30 min Continuous effort / contract Incident command, safety/security, executive, Level 3 engineering, field
S2 High Major robot/site degradation; critical mission blocked; limited safe workaround 30 min 60 min 4 hours or contract Duty manager, Level 2, field/Level 3
S3 Medium Single robot degraded; workaround available; limited impact 4 business hours Daily/as agreed 2 business days Queue lead/Level 2
S4 Low Question, cosmetic, documentation, enhancement 1 business day On material change Planned Standard queue/product feedback

Response is not resolution. Track time to safe state, restore, permanent fix, and customer confirmation separately. Pause rules must be contractual and auditable.

Escalation triggers independent of severity

Repeat incident across robots/sites; suspected fleet-wide version issue; near miss; unknown safe state; support loses required telemetry; remote access anomaly; part stockout threatening the SLA; customer executive escalation; acceptance/hypercare blocker; public/social/media exposure.
