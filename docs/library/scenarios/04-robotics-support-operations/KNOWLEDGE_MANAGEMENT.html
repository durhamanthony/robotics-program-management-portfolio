Knowledge Management — Robotics Support

Content types

Customer article, L1 script, L2 diagnostic runbook, field procedure, safety notice, known-error/workaround, release note, training module, escalation checklist, and post-incident learning.

Required metadata

Title, product/model, version/configuration, audience, symptoms/fault, safety warnings, prerequisites/authorization, steps, expected result, escalation/rollback, evidence, owner, approver, status, effective/review/expiry dates, source case/problem/change.

Workflow

Draft from validated resolution → technical and safety/security review as applicable → controlled publish → link from case/tool → collect usefulness and resolution feedback → update when product/process changes → retire or archive. Search uses customer language, fault codes and symptoms.

Measures

Knowledge use, successful self-service/assisted resolution, escalation rate, article feedback, content age, review compliance, search failures, duplicate content, cases eligible for new/updated content, and unsafe/outdated article defects. AI-assisted drafts require source traceability and human approval before operational use.
