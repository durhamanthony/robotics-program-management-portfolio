Customer Communications — Robotics Support

Evidence-confidence key: [PB-H] public benchmark or authoritative source/high confidence; [RBE-M] range-based estimate/medium confidence; [SA-L] scenario assumption/low confidence; [DC-L] disclosed derived calculation/confidence inherits the weakest input; [UPV] unverified production value/block commitment until approved.

Incident update template

Subject: [Severity] [site/robot or service] — [confirmed impact]
Status: investigating / mitigating / monitoring / resolved
Confirmed facts: what is known, including affected scope and safety state
Actions: completed and underway
Customer action: required or none
Next update: exact time/time zone
Owner/case: named contact and correlation ID

Do not speculate on cause, promise unapproved recovery times, disclose other customers, or describe a robot as safe without qualified confirmation.

Cadence

Meet the contractual severity cadence even when there is no material change; say what is still being tested. Separate technical work from the communication clock. Provide resolution summary, remaining risk/workaround, prevention action and monitoring plan after closure.

Planned communication

Release/update notice states value, affected models/versions, prerequisites, maintenance window, expected behavior, known limitations, rollback, support route and approval. QBRs cover outcomes, reliability, incidents/problems, support performance, parts/field trends, roadmap, risks and mutually owned actions.
