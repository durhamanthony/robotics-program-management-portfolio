Staffing and Capacity Plan — Robotics Support

Evidence-confidence key: [PB-H] public benchmark or authoritative source/high confidence; [RBE-M] range-based estimate/medium confidence; [SA-L] scenario assumption/low confidence; [DC-L] disclosed derived calculation/confidence inherits the weakest input; [UPV] unverified production value/block commitment until approved.

Demand model

Monthly workload = installed robots × contacts/robot + telemetry-created actionable incidents + planned maintenance + deployments/updates + field work + problem/knowledge/admin demand. Segment by model, site maturity, version, severity, geography, skill and coverage window.

Capacity controls

Forecast 13 weeks and 12 months; maintain coverage/skills matrix and on-call sustainability; reserve launch surge capacity; define partner qualifications; cross-train critical skills; cap sustained planned utilization at 75%; measure non-case work; and review regional travel and part locations.
