Customer Discovery Plan — AD-01

Questions to answer

Is the workflow frequent and costly enough; which tote/station/layout envelope repeats; who experiences, owns, funds, integrates, approves, operates, supports, and blocks the purchase; what alternatives exist; what proof is required; and what commercial/service model is acceptable?

Evidence plan

Activity Target Output
Stakeholder interviews 15 across at least 5 companies Problem, workflow, buying and risk themes
Site observations 3 representative facilities Time study, layout, payload and exception data
Concept reviews 5 buying teams Ranked outcomes, objections, proof needs
Workflow prototype 3 design partners Task success, intervention and integration findings
Commercial test 3 budget owners Price/Total Cost of Ownership sensitivity and contract expectations

Interview discipline

Ask for recent examples, actual volumes/costs, failure cases, workarounds, decision process, and existing budgets. Do not lead with a robot demo or treat enthusiasm as commitment. Record hypotheses separately from evidence and remove customer-identifying information from portfolio material.

Exit criteria

At least three design partners validate a common narrow workflow; measurable current-state baseline exists; critical requirements and non-goals are understood; economic buyer and approval path are known; engineering deems the envelope feasible; and one qualified first customer is willing to contract with explicit acceptance and limitation language.
