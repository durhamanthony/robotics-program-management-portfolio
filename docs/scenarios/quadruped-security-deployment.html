CASE 02 · Security operations deployment

Three Quadrupeds — Night Security

Sentinel Ridge Industrial Campus · Security Robotics Program Manager

Closed — 30-night pilot accepted20 weeks plus 30-night pilot$730K installed$1.315M five-year TCO
Executive view

Program outcome and acceptance

Green

Scope

Deploy three quadruped robots for human-supervised night patrol across stairs, curbs, gravel, and narrow walkways. Two robots run scheduled perimeter routes while the third rotates through charging and reserve coverage. Robots observe and report; authorized human security personnel retain all response decisions.

Closeout decision

The pilot met all safety, privacy, patrol, alert, geofence, availability, and docking criteria. Sentinel Ridge authorized two recurring quadruped patrol posts and retained the existing fixed-post responder. Expansion beyond the approved routes requires a new route, privacy, network, and benefit review.

Decision rule and confidence limits

The May 2025 U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics national mean wage for security guards is $20.42 per hour [PB-H]; it is only a wage benchmark. The fictional $45 contractor invoice is 2.20 times that wage because it is assumed to include burden, supervision, insurance, overhead, and margin [SA-L]. With two 10-hour rover posts and a $15K vehicle, payback is 3.22 years, discounted payback is 3.88 years, and five-year Net Present Value at 8% is $174,099 [DC-L]. The five-year break-even invoice rate is $33.98/hour. At one removed post, payback is 11.73 years and the decision fails. Actual invoices, contract-change rights, routes, costs, and realized savings are [UPV].

Formal closure

Acceptance, handoff, and lessons

ScheduleCompleted in week 20 after a 30-night pilot
Budget$730,250 spent against $730,250 authorization

Acceptance and handoff evidence

  • All scheduled patrol and availability thresholds passed
  • Fifty witnessed critical alerts arrived within 15 seconds
  • Privacy masking, access, retention, deletion, and audit tests passed
  • Network-loss, sensor-degradation, low-battery, docking, and retrieval tests passed
  • Human responders retained authority for every alert disposition

Lessons carried forward

  • Survey the route at the actual patrol hour and weather conditions
  • Measure false-alert workload before unattended patrol
  • Design evidence and support correlation together
  • Return every route or sensor expansion to privacy, safety, network, and financial gates
