Robotics Support & Field Operations

Orion Field Robotics · Service Operations Program Manager

Executive view

Program outcome and acceptance

Green

Scope

Stand up one traceable support chain for 250 humanoid and quadruped robots across 60 sites. The operating model connects Salesforce customer cases, ServiceNow or Zendesk workflows, field work, Jira engineering defects, robot telemetry, parts, knowledge, release readiness, and customer communications.

Closeout decision

The executive sponsor and service owner accepted the 180-day operating model. All 250 robots and 60 sites have owned installed-product records; severity, on-call, field, parts, remote-access, continuity, and product-readiness controls are live. High-confidence predictive case automation remains a phase-two improvement and is not required for closeout.

How to read these numbers

Financial figures are planning-grade unless explicitly identified as an actual result or binding quotation.

Public benchmark Published external reference used only as a reasonableness check. Research-based estimate Best estimate informed by published evidence, but not customer- or vendor-confirmed. Scenario assumption Fictional input selected to make the demonstration model complete. Derived calculation Reproducible arithmetic calculated from identified inputs. Unknown / pending validation Requires a time study, customer data, competitive quote, or contract.

Confidence: High = authoritative source or controlled fact; Medium = reasoned estimate/control design; Low = fictional or unvalidated scenario input. A calculation inherits its least-certain material input.

Decision rule and confidence limits

The arithmetic is reconciled, but the 250-robot installed base, demand rates, staffing costs, and platform amounts are fictional planning inputs. The year-one authorization includes the six-month implementation, initial 15-person operation, platform integration, telemetry, remote access, field tools, starter spares, enablement, contingency, and sponsor reserve. Vendor prices and actual workload remain quote- and evidence-required.

Acceptance, handoff, and lessons

ScheduleCompleted on day 180
Budget$4,412,000 spent against $4,668,000 authorization; $256,000 reserve returned

Acceptance and handoff evidence

  • All 250 robot records linked to site, version, entitlement, and owner
  • Severity 1 and Severity 2 on-call and customer-communication drills passed
  • Case, work order, Jira defect, and telemetry correlation demonstrated end to end
  • Field dispatch, critical spare, and return-to-service exercises passed
  • Service owner accepted Key Performance Indicator dashboard, staffing triggers, continuity plan, and phase-two roadmap

Lessons carried forward

  • Installed-product data quality is a release dependency
  • Customer communication and diagnosis need parallel owners
  • Automate alerts only after precision and ownership are measured
  • Support evidence must feed product reliability and serviceability decisions
