Acceptance and handoff evidence

  • 360 of 360 device assignments have accepted final disposition
  • New build, security, app, data, and user evidence accepted
  • Old assets reconcile through custody and sanitization/disposition
  • Service, vendor, financial, risks, lessons, and benefits transferred

Lessons carried forward

  • Readiness and downstream capacity must govern wave size
  • A device is not Done at handoff; old-asset disposition closes the loop
  • Specialized personas and remote returns need explicit capacity and earlier discovery
