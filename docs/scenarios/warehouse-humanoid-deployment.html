CASE 01 · Customer deployment

Five Humanoids — Warehouse Truck Loading

Northstar Distribution Center · Deployment Program Manager

Closed — accepted for controlled two-shift operation24 weeks$1.346M installed$2.226M five-year TCO
Executive view

Program outcome and acceptance

Green

Scope

Deliver five humanoids from signed order through site acceptance, controlled pilot, truck-loading cutover, hypercare, and operations handoff. Each robot works in a separate lane, receives an approved carton at a conveyor, and places it on a floor pallet. A robot-operated forklift loads each completed seven-carton pallet into an outbound truck.

Closeout decision

Northstar accepted the cell for controlled two-shift use after all mandatory safety tests passed and the 14-day hypercare window exceeded the contracted throughput, mission-success, intervention, damage, and availability thresholds. A second cell remains conditional on Finance validating at least $445,200 of annual gross capacity value using actual labor and volume data.

Decision rule

Formal closure

Acceptance, handoff, and lessons

ScheduleCompleted in week 24
Budget$1,346,000 spent against $1,346,000 authorization

Acceptance and handoff evidence

  • All mandatory safety tests passed with no open critical findings
  • Thirty full material-flow cycles completed
  • Two-hour throughput and quality test passed
  • Operators and support staff completed role assessment
  • Telemetry-to-case escalation and return-to-service drill passed

Lessons carried forward

  • Freeze the representative carton set before Factory Acceptance Testing
  • Treat trailer wireless coverage as a site-readiness gate
  • Put diagnostic evidence requirements into the released configuration
  • Link expansion to finance-approved capacity value, not animation speed
