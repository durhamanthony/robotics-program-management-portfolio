MuJoCo Simulation Gallery

All six clips on this page are rendered from MuJoCo models in this repository. The motions are scripted operational visualizations—not learned locomotion, grasp-performance validation, certified controls, or vendor digital twins. Recruiters and hiring managers can play them directly in the browser without Python, a terminal, or MuJoCo.

CASE 01

MuJoCo Retail Backroom Humanoid Simulation

Rendered from a MuJoCo backroom model with human-scale aisles, a receiving area, garment rack, four-step stock platform, shoe shelving, tablet station, and employee handoff. Scripted robot motion shows the workflow; it is not locomotion, grasp, or safety-performance validation.

CASE 02

MuJoCo Quadruped Security-Site Simulation

Rendered from a MuJoCo fenced industrial site with a building, guard booth, vehicle gate, pavement, rough-ground test area, steps, loading platform, lights, and charging docks. The earlier colored route lines were removed; robot position and the physical site now show the patrol sequence directly.

CASE 03

MuJoCo AD-01 Autonomous Tote Transfer Robot

AD-01 means Autonomous Delivery, product concept 01. It is the fictional product developed in Case 03: a compact wheeled mobile manipulator for one approved tote up to 15 kilograms on an 18-meter indoor route between two fixed stations. This clip is rendered from its actuated MuJoCo model.

CASE 04

MuJoCo Robotics Field-Service Bay Simulation

Rendered from a MuJoCo field-service bay with a faulted quadruped, technician, diagnostic laptop, spare module, status lights, and verification zone. The dashboard and artifacts carry the broader case, telemetry, escalation, dispatch, and customer-communication workflow.
