CASE 01 · MuJoCo operations visualization

MuJoCo Retail Backroom Humanoid Simulation

A 40-second, seven-stage MuJoCo retail-backroom story. A robot-operated forklift backs a full pallet out of the delivery truck and parks it in receiving. After the forklift clears the zone, two humanoids carry cartons to lower and raised storage racks; the upper-route robot visibly climbs each of four physical treads. The scene then cuts to order fulfillment: both robots pull requested merchandise and place it on the courtesy drop-off table for a sales associate. On-video stage labels make the workflow explicit. Scripted motion is not locomotion, grasp, forklift-control, collision-avoidance, or safety-performance validation. [DC-L; Low confidence]
