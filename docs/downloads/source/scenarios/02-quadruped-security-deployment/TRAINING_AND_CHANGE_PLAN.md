**Table 1. Training and Change Plan — Night Security — Evidence: disclosed row/source notes; Confidence: see evidence key and row/source notes**

| Audience | Required competence | Pass evidence |
|---|---|---|
| SOC operators | Mission monitoring, alert verification, privacy, safe hold/return, escalation | Simulator scenarios + quiz |
| Supervisors | Go/no-go for weather/site changes; incident command; evidence approval | Tabletop exercise |
| Human responders | Robot limitations; location/evidence interpretation; no reliance without verification | Response drill |

| IT/security/privacy | Identity, network, logging, retention, access review, incident handling | Control walkthrough |

Notify affected staff and approved stakeholders before data collection. Begin with escorted shadow patrols. Publish short job aids at the console and dock. Hold daily feedback during hypercare and tune only through approved change control. Recertify after major route, sensor, software, policy, or recovery-procedure changes.
