# Closeout and Maturity Roadmap — Robotics Support

**Company:** Orion Field Robotics  
**Program duration:** 180 days  
**Installed base:** 250 robots at 60 customer sites  
**Final decision:** Service owner accepted the operating model  
**Year-one authorization / final spend:** $4,668,000 / $4,412,000; $256,000 reserve returned

## Final operating results

| Measure | Target | Day-180 result | Disposition |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Installed-product records | 100% of 250 robots | 250 of 250 | Pass |
| Complete escalation evidence | At least 95% | 96% | Pass |
| Remote resolution for eligible cases | At least 65% | 68% | Pass |
| First-time field fix | At least 85% | 87% | Pass |
| Severity 1 acknowledgement | Within 15 minutes | 12-minute median | Pass |
| Foundation backlog | 14 capabilities | 14 accepted | Pass |

Salesforce cases, the service workflow, Jira engineering defects, field work, and robot telemetry now share a robot identity and correlation identifier. Severity, on-call, customer communication, safe remote access, field dispatch, critical parts, Return Material Authorization (RMA), return-to-service, knowledge, continuity, and release-readiness controls were exercised before handoff.

## Closeout decision

The service owner accepted the operating model and its Key Performance Indicator (KPI), capacity, financial, continuity, and product-feedback controls. High-confidence predictive alert-to-case automation remains a phase-two improvement. It was deliberately excluded from the closure gate until alert precision and ownership are proven.

## Maturity roadmap

| Level | Characteristics | Next evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Reactive | Person-dependent, fragmented intake, weak asset/evidence data | Single intake, severity, installed-base baseline |
| 2 Repeatable | Standard triage, linked cases/defects/work orders, basic KPIs | Compliance and quality trends |
| 3 Managed | Telemetry, problem management, field/parts capacity, readiness gates | Predictive reliability and economics |
| 4 Scalable | Multi-site automation, version/config segmentation, service productization | Proven automation precision and controls |
| 5 Learning | Closed-loop reliability/serviceability and portfolio optimization | Measured customer/product outcomes |

## Benefits review

At 30, 60, and 90 days after handoff, the service owner reviews safe-response compliance, Service-Level Agreement (SLA) and customer communication performance, evidence completeness, remote resolution, first-time fix, repeat incidents, availability and mission impact, parts fill, cost per robot per month, customer satisfaction, workforce health, and product corrective actions. Any automation or cost reduction must preserve safety, security, privacy, and evidence quality.
