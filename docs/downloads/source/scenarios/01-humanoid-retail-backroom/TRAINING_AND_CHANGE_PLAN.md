# Training and Change Plan — Retail Backroom Pilot

## Change objective

Keep associates on the customer floor while the robot handles approved backroom retrieval. The message is specific: this pilot changes how an item request is fulfilled; it does not authorize customer-floor operation, remove emergency authority from employees, or guarantee chain rollout.

## Role curriculum and qualification

| Audience | Content | Practice | Pass criterion | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales associates | submit/cancel request; choose handoff; confirm item | 10 requests including one rejection | 9/10 correct and all safety questions correct | Change Lead |
| Backroom associates | right-of-way; exception zone; blocked route; stock correction | five normal and five exception cases | no control bypass; 90% knowledge score | Store Operations |
| Supervisors | pause/restart authority; incident; staffing; KPI review | tabletop plus live recovery | all critical decisions correct | Safety Lead |
| Support analysts | alert correlation; logs; remote evidence; escalation | three seeded faults | correct severity, case data, and escalation | Service Owner |
| Field technicians | isolation, recovery, replaceable units, post-repair test | hands-on repair drill | restore inside target with safety checklist | Field Service |

## Adoption measures

Training completion 100%; role-assessment pass at least 90%; tablet-request adoption at least 85% of eligible requests by pilot week 2; rejected/abandoned requests below 5%; weekly employee feedback with owner/date on every action.

## Communications

Week 3 sponsor/store briefing; week 8 manager demonstration; week 10 published operating rules; weeks 13–14 shift training; daily pilot huddle; weekly KPI/feedback review; week 20 closeout and financial decision. Employees may stop work and report hazards without penalty.
