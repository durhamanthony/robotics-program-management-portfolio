## Benefit accounting rules

- Do not count capacity as savings unless Finance confirms a realized budget change.
- Do not claim exposure reduction from simulation; use verified incident and task data.
- Do not monetize passenger experience without an approved valuation method.
- Report sample size and denominator with every percentage.
- Keep pilot acceptance separate from expansion economics.

## Scale gate

The pilot passed service and control thresholds but did not establish economic scale. The Benefits Owner recommended no rollout until the airport measures a broader workload, obtains quotations, and identifies non-labor value that can be verified without double counting.
