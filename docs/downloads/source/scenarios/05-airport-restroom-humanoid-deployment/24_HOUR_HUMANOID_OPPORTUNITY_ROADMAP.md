# 24-Hour Humanoid Opportunity Roadmap

## Selection rule

Prioritize work where a human-form machine can reuse doors, stairs, carts, shelves, tools, and human-height interfaces, and where demand is dense enough to keep the robot productive. Do not use a humanoid merely because a task occurs around the clock.

| Rank | Operating area | Humanoid fit | First measurable workflow | Portfolio position |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Three-shift manufacturing logistics | Very high | Sequencing, cart movement, and line-side replenishment | Strongest commercial evidence |
| 2 | Airport integrated facilities | High | Restroom service plus approved replenishment, waste, and inspection tasks | Recommended Phase 2 |
| 3 | Airport irregular baggage handling | High but controlled | Human-scale lifting and handling of exceptions | Safety and regulatory discovery required |
| 4 | Hospital nonclinical logistics | Medium-high | Supply, linen, waste, and equipment movement | Compare against wheeled automation first |
| 5 | Retail and grocery backroom | Medium-high | Mixed-item retrieval, replenishment, and overnight recovery | Existing portfolio scenario |
| 6 | Hotel and resort back-of-house | Medium | Linen, amenities, waste, and facilities rounds | Validate utilization density |

## Disqualification rule

A wheeled autonomous mobile robot, fixed arm, conveyor, or conventional scrubber should win when it can safely complete the same approved workflow at lower lifecycle cost. Flat-floor point-to-point transport, generic open-floor scrubbing, and low-density inspection are not strong humanoid cases by themselves.

## Evidence used for opportunity ranking

- Agility Robotics and GXO reported a formal multi-year Robotics-as-a-Service deployment for tote movement in a live warehouse.
- Figure reported 10-hour shifts and production logistics work at BMW, and separately described cart and component sequencing using existing workflows.
- Agility Robotics announced a commercial agreement with Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada.
- Agility Robotics reported more than 100,000 totes moved in Mercado Libre operations using existing aisles and workflows.
- Figure announced a retail-distribution agreement with Catalyst Brands.

These are vendor-reported commercial or operating examples. They support workflow plausibility, not the airport scenario's cost, safety, quality, or ROI assumptions.
