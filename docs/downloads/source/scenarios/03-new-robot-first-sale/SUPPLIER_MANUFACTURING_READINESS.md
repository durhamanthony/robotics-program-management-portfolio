# Supplier and Manufacturing Readiness — AD-01

## Supplier controls

Critical items have approved specifications, supplier qualification, lead time/Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ), capacity, change-notification obligation, quality agreement, traceability, end-of-life plan, and second-source or approved risk acceptance. Long-lead and single-source exposure is reviewed at every gate.

## Manufacturing readiness checklist

| Area | PVT exit evidence |
|---|---|
| Bill of Materials/configuration | Released Bill of Materials (BOM), approved alternates, serialized hardware/software baseline |
| Process | Work instructions, tooling, fixtures, torque/calibration controls, training |
| Quality | Incoming/in-process/end-of-line tests, nonconformance/Material Review Board (MRB), traceability |
| Capacity | Demonstrated rate, takt/cycle time, labor/equipment plan, bottleneck response |
| Yield | First-pass and final yield meet approved targets with root causes for losses |
| Supply | Critical inventory, supplier capacity, logistics/packaging, shortage plan |
| Release | Device identity, software load, keys/certificates, audit and rollback |
| Service | Field-Replaceable Units (FRUs), diagnostics, spare allocation, repair/Return Material Authorization (RMA), and field procedures |

## Stop-ship criteria

Open critical safety/security defect; unknown configuration; failed required test; unapproved deviation; missing traceability; uncontained supplier nonconformance; or absent first-customer/service readiness. Waivers require named authority, scope, expiration, risk, controls, and customer disclosure when applicable.
