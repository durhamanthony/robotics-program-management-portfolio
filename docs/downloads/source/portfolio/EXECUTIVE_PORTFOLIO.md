# Case 1 — Two humanoids in a retail backroom

**Program outcome:** pilot employee-facing shoe-box and hanging-garment retrieval in an unchanged legacy department-store backroom, then separate operational acceptance from the financial scale decision.

The project package includes a charter and Statement of Work (SOW), business case and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), basis of estimate, schedule, budget, Responsibility Assignment Matrix (RACI), risk/assumption/issue/dependency register (RAID), requirements traceability, site and integration readiness, safety and acceptance, training, deployment runbook, weekly status, operations handoff, and benefits review. The workflow covers tablet requests, existing aisles and doors, a short stair, approved item retrieval, employee handoff, and inventory confirmation. Tangled hangers, ladders, electrical work, and customer-floor activity remain human exceptions.

**PM decisions:** validate the item and operating envelope; define stop and recovery behavior; assign customer/vendor interfaces; block go-live on critical readiness gaps; stage Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), Site Acceptance Testing (SAT), and User Acceptance Testing (UAT); tie support and telemetry to go-live; and refuse chain rollout when the accepted pilot does not clear the financial gate.

# Case 2 — Three quadrupeds for night security

**Program outcome:** operate approved patrol routes with human verification, privacy governance, safe return/docking, evidence handling, and field/support coverage.

The package includes SOW/business case, route and site readiness, requirements, privacy/safety acceptance, alert/data integration, human-in-the-loop incident response, training, cost/control artifacts, operations runbook, and benefits review.

**Boundary:** robots observe and report. They do not confront, pursue, detain, use force, or make unsupervised law-enforcement decisions.

# Case 3 — AD-01 through first sale

**Program outcome:** move a fictional compact mobile manipulator from validated customer need through Engineering Verification Testing (EVT), Design Verification Testing (DVT), Production Validation Testing (PVT), first shipment, acceptance, and the General Availability (GA) decision.

The package includes discovery, product brief/PRD, roadmap, requirements traceability, business case, integrated stage gates, supplier/manufacturing readiness, V&V, reliability/serviceability, budget/headcount, commercial launch, first-customer plan, status reporting, and lessons.

**Program principle:** narrow the first-release envelope and retire safety, technical, supplier, manufacturing, service, and commercial risk with evidence before funding the next gate.

# Case 4 — Robotics support operations

**Program outcome:** create one traceable chain from customer or telemetry signal to safe restoration, field/RMA work, engineering correction, knowledge, and reliability improvement.

The service design covers L0/L1/L2/field/L3 roles, severity/SLA, incident/problem/change, installed product and configuration, MCAP/Foxglove evidence, fleet observability, Grafana/Prometheus, safe remote access, OTA, knowledge, field capacity, parts/RMA, customer communications, continuity, KPIs, cost, and a 180-day maturity roadmap.

# Operating model

Across all scenarios I use lifecycle gates, measurable outcomes, requirements-to-test traceability, safety/security/privacy escalation, configuration/change control, decision/action/RAID logs, vendor and budget controls, training/adoption, deployment/operations handoff, and 30/60/90-day benefits reviews.
